# Chapter 1: The Hidden Mark

The raking light of the Louvre’s skylights illuminated the intricate brushstrokes of the Renaissance masterpiece before Isabelle Moreau. As an art authenticator, she’d scrutinized thousands of paintings, but this one, a portrait of a forgotten nobleman, held an unusual allure.  A faint irregularity in the paint texture near the subject’s hand caught her eye.  Leaning closer, her breath ghosting over the aged varnish, Isabelle traced the subtle bump with her fingertip. It felt like a deliberate indentation, hidden beneath layers of paint. 

Back in her workshop, under the magnified gaze of her specialized lamp, Isabelle carefully applied a solvent to a small section of the canvas.  As the varnish dissolved, a faint, almost imperceptible symbol emerged—a stylized serpent coiled around a dagger.  A chill snaked down her spine. This was no accidental imperfection. This was a message, a secret encoded within the art itself.  

A sharp rap at her door broke the spell. A tall, imposing figure filled the frame, his dark eyes scrutinizing her with an intensity that made her pulse quicken. “Isabelle Moreau?” His voice was a low rumble, edged with authority. “Detective Adrian Villalobos, Parisian Police.”

Isabelle’s apprehension spiked. “Is something wrong?”

“We have reason to believe you may have information pertinent to an ongoing investigation.”  He stepped into the workshop, his gaze sweeping across the array of tools and canvases. “A smuggling operation.”

Smuggling? What could her work possibly have to do with smugglers?  Isabelle felt a knot tighten in her stomach.  “I’m afraid I don’t understand.”

Adrian produced a photograph, placing it on her workbench. The image showed a crate filled with confiscated artifacts, and etched into the wood, clear as day, was the same serpent and dagger symbol she’d just uncovered. The blood drained from her face. This was more than a coincidence. Much more.
