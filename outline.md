Yield it peacefully, and avoid bloodshed."  Her chin lifted.  "My family has tilled this land for generations, Laird. You will not take it!" His lips curled into a sneer. A marriage to save her family, a union with her sworn enemy… It was a terrifying prospect, yet perhaps, the only option.
